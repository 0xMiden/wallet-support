For a transfer within Miden, both wallets must use the same network. A transfer to another chain needs a supported crosschain route.

**To receive:**

Open **Receive** and share the account address or QR code for the intended network. Share the address, never your recovery phrase or signing keys. Ask the sender to check the network and token. Incoming transfers may need to be accepted in **Activity** before they become spendable. A very small balance can be hidden from **Home**. See [*Why don't I see a token on Home?*](#common-issues-and-support/why-dont-i-see-a-token-on-home)

**To send:**

Open **Send**, enter or select the recipient, choose the token and amount, then review the network, address, amount, fee and any expiration before confirming. Keep enough of the displayed fee asset for the transaction. If the address or network is unexpected, stop and check with the recipient. If the review shows **New recipient** beside **To**, you haven't sent to it before and it isn't a saved contact or one of your accounts: check it with the recipient before you confirm. **Max** sends your whole spendable balance.

Track the send in **Activity**. A confirmed send can still require the recipient to accept the transfer. See [*My transfer shows as confirmed, but the token never arrived.*](#common-issues-and-support/my-transfer-shows-as-completed-but-the-token-never-arrived)
