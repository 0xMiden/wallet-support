mainCategory: cross-chain

---

Only use a cross-chain route when your installed Bread version offers it for the selected network and asset.

On Testnet, use only the displayed test networks and test assets. Never send real funds to a test address. A Sepolia route is not an Ethereum Mainnet route.

On Mainnet, use real assets only after your installed version supports Mainnet and shows the exact route. Check the source chain, destination chain, token, recipient and fees before you confirm. Store updates and route availability can differ by platform.

Keep the resulting total wallet balance within the Partner Mainnet cap. See [*What is the Partner Mainnet deposit limit?*](#moving-across-chains/what-is-the-partner-mainnet-deposit-limit) For route differences, see [*What is the difference between a solver route and a canonical bridge?*](#moving-across-chains/what-is-the-difference-between-a-solver-route-and-a-canonical-bridge)

For the steps on mobile, see [*How do I send funds from Bread to another blockchain?*](#moving-across-chains/how-do-i-send-funds-from-bread-to-another-blockchain)
