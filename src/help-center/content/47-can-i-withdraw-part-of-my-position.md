title: Can I withdraw part of my position?
mainCategory: earning
subcategory: earn
platforms: [extension-desktop, mobile]

No. A withdrawal returns your whole position. The review page shows **Withdrawal** as **Full position (gasless)**.

To keep some funds earning, withdraw and then deposit the amount you want to keep. See [*How do I withdraw from Earn?*](#earn/how-do-i-withdraw-from-earn)
