title: Can I withdraw part of my position?
mainCategory: earning

Earn depends on your installed version, network, eligibility and an available provider quote. If a control is disabled or a quote is unavailable, do not submit a new transaction. See [*Can I earn yield in Bread?*](#earn/can-i-earn-yield-in-bread)

Yes, on a supported route with a valid quote. Select **Withdraw**, enter an amount up to the displayed available balance, then select **Review**. Use **Max** if you want to withdraw the available balance instead.

The review shows **You withdraw** and **Stays invested**, so you can check how much remains in the position. The quoted receipt can be lower than the selected amount because of route costs. Check the expected and minimum receipt before confirming.

A small amount may not have a usable quote. Do not exceed the available balance or start another withdrawal while one from the same position is still in progress. See [*How do I withdraw from Earn?*](#earn/how-do-i-withdraw-from-earn)
