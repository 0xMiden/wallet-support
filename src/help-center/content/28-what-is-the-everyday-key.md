title: What is the everyday key?
mainCategory: guardian
subcategory: guardian-protection
platforms: [extension-desktop, mobile]
---

The everyday key lives on your device and signs your regular transactions. When you click **Send**, it signs on your device, protected by your wallet password or biometrics.

Sending, swapping and earning use the everyday key plus Guardian. The everyday key can't move funds on its own.

If you think your device is compromised, use **Rotate everyday key** to replace it. If you lose your device, restoring your wallet sets up a new everyday key. See [*Can I get my wallet back if I lose my device?*](#security-and-recovery/can-i-get-my-wallet-back-if-i-lose-my-device)
