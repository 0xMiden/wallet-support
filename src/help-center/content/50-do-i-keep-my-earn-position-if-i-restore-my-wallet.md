title: Do I keep my Earn position if I restore my wallet?
mainCategory: earning
subcategory: earn
platforms: [extension-desktop, mobile]
---

Yes, if your wallet is backed up with Guardian. Restore the wallet and your position appears again under **Current Positions** on the **Earn** tab.

Without Guardian, losing your device means losing the wallet and the position with it.

To restore, see [*How do I restore my wallet with a recovery phrase?*](#security-and-recovery/how-do-i-restore-my-wallet-with-a-recovery-phrase)
