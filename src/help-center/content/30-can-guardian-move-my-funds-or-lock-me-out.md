title: Can Guardian move my funds or lock me out?
mainCategory: guardian
subcategory: guardian-protection
platforms: [extension-desktop, mobile]
---

No, on both counts.

Every action needs two of the three keys, so no single key can move funds alone. Guardian holds only one key, which means it acknowledges state updates but can never act on its own.

You hold the other two: the everyday key and the emergency key. You can walk away from Guardian at any time using your own two keys. See [*How do I switch Guardian operators?*](#guardian-protection/how-do-i-switch-guardian-operators)

For the full picture of why and how Guardian coordinates without ever taking custody, see [*What is Guardian?*](#guardian-protection/what-is-guardian)
