title: Can I get my wallet back if I lose my device?
mainCategory: manage-wallet
subcategory: security-and-recovery
platforms: [extension-desktop, mobile]
---

Yes. Restore Bread on a new device with your recovery phrase, and your Guardian's backup brings back your account. See [*How do I restore my wallet with a recovery phrase?*](#security-and-recovery/how-do-i-restore-my-wallet-with-a-recovery-phrase)

You need your recovery phrase for this. Guardian can't replace it. If you lose the phrase and every device with your wallet on it, Guardian can't recover your funds for you.

Your account works on one device at a time. Restoring it on a new device makes that device the only one with access.
