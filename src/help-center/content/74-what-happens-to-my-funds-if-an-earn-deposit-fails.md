title: What happens to my funds if an Earn deposit fails?
mainCategory: earning
subcategory: earn
platforms: [extension-desktop, mobile]
---

A failed Earn deposit can't be retried. If your funds left your account before the deposit failed, they come back to you after a waiting period. Don't make another deposit to get them back.

Epoch, the lending service that Earn uses, sets the waiting period. On Mainnet it is currently 6,200 Miden blocks, which Bread estimates at about 5 hours. Epoch can change it.

1. Open **Activity** and check the deposit. Let the wallet sync.
2. After the waiting period, the USDCX comes back to your balance. Bread accepts it automatically when **Auto-accept USDCX transfers** is on in **General** settings, which it is unless you turned it off. If the setting is off, accept the pending transfer on the **Activity** page. See [*How do I accept a pending transfer?*](#activity-and-transaction-status/how-do-i-accept-a-pending-transfer)
3. To deposit again, start a new deposit from the **Earn** tab. See [*How do I deposit into Earn?*](#earn/how-do-i-deposit-into-earn)

If the funds haven't come back long after the waiting period, contact [support](/feedback).
